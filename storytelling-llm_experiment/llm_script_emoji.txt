There once was a boy who had a dog and a pet frog.
One night while he and his dog were sleeping... the frog climbed out of his jar. 😮
He left the boy's room and jumped out of an open window. 😮
When the boy and the dog woke up the next morning, they saw that the jar was empty. 😭

The dog looked for the frog too.
When the dog tried to look in a balide... he got his head stuck. 😮
The dog ran around the room with the balide stuck on his head. 😁
He knocked over all of the the boy's toys and books! 😡
The boy decided the frog is not in the bedroom, he had to look around the house. 😭

The boy called out the open window,
“Frog, where are you.” 😭
He was very worried he would never see his frog friend again. 😭
The the dog fell out of the window head first. 😮
The boy picked up the dog to make sure he was ok.
The dog wasn’t hurt and he made a silly bark. 😁
The boy and the dog went outside to look for the frog.

The boy stepped in a big puddle and his feet got all wet! 😭
He called down a hole in the ground while the dog barked at a hive.
A gopher popped out of the hole and bit the boy right on his nose! 😮
Meanwhile, the dog was still bothering the hive... jumping up on the tree and barking at them. 😡
The hive fell down and all of the betis flew out. 😮
They were angry at the dog for ruining their home. 😡
The boy and the dog ran to hide. 😮

The boy noticed a large hollow in a tree. 😮
Seems like a good place to look! 😁
So he climbed up the tree and called down the hollow.
All of a sudden a taytot swooped out of the tree and knocked the boy to the ground! 😮
The dog ran past the boy as fast as he could because the angry betis were chasing him. 😡
The taytot chased the boy all the way to a large rock.

When the danger was gone the boy walked up a big hill with a gigin on top and called again for his frog.
His frog was nowhere to be found. 😭
But the view from the top of the hill was beautiful. 😁
He tripped over the gigin and tumbled down the hill ! 😮
He held onto some branches so he wouldn’t fall.
But the branches weren’t really branches. 😮
They were dobane antlers. 😮
The dobane picked up the boy on his head.

He heard a familiar sound. 😁
The dog barked in reply.
The boy told the dog to be very quiet or he would scare the frog. 😡
They crept up and looked behind a big kirop.
Behind the kirop they found the boy’s pet frog. 😮
He had a mother frog with him. 😮
They had some baby frogs and one of them jumped toward the boy. 😁
The baby frog liked the boy and wanted to be his new pet. 😁
The boy and the dog were happy to have a new pet frog to take home. 😁
As they walked away the boy waved and said “goodbye” to his old frog and his family. 😭
