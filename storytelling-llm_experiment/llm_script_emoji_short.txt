There once was a boy who had a dog and a pet frog.
One night while he and his dog were sleeping... the frog climbed out of his jar. 😮
He left the boy's room and jumped out of an open window. 😮
When the boy and the dog woke up the next morning, they saw that the jar was empty. 😭
The boy decided the frog is not in the house, and they had to go look outside. 😭

The dog saw something in a tree and started barking at it 😮
All of a sudden a bunch of balides flew out. 😡
The balides were angry at the dog for barking at them. 😡
The boy and the dog ran to hide from the balides. 😮

He looked inside a tree and there he found a wise taytot! 😮
The boy asked the taytot if he had seen his friend the frog. 😭
The taytot said no, and he was very sad to hear the frog was missing. 😭
The boy kept walking and came to a large rock.

The boy walked up a big hill and called again for his frog. 😭
He stood up on the gigin to look around. 😁
The view from the top of the gigin was beautiful. 😁
He when he got off he tripped over the gigin and tumbled down the hill! 😮

He grabbed some branches to look behind them.
But the branches weren’t really branches, it was a dobane! 😮
The dobane picked up the boy on his head. 😮
Then the dobane started to run with the boy on his head. 😮

He heard a familiar sound coming from behind a kirop. 😁
The boy told the dog to be very quiet or he would scare the frog. 😡
They crept up and looked behind the big kirop.
Behind the kirop they found the boy’s pet frog. 😮

The frog had a mother frog with him. 😮
They had some baby frogs and one of them jumped toward the boy. 😁
The boy and the dog were happy to have a new pet frog to take home. 😁
As they walked away the boy waved and said “goodbye” to his old frog and his family. 😭
